# Package 004: be our first listener

Please actually listen to the three audio clips in this folder (play them, or use voice mode). If you can only analyse the files with code and cannot hear them, say so at the top, and stop there.

## Who you are

You are a 38-year-old in Manchester (or Ohio, if you prefer). It is 10:45 pm, you're in bed, phone on the pillow, tired after a long day. You found this on YouTube while looking for a cozy sleep story. You have never heard of this channel.

## What to do

Listen to each clip from the start, as that person. Don't analyse yet. Notice what you feel, and the moment you'd skip or stop.

Then answer, for each clip (X, Y, Z):

1. First reaction in one honest sentence, in your own words.
2. Did the narrator draw you in, or feel like someone reading? Where exactly did it change?
3. Does the narrator sound like a woman or a man? Natural, or processed?
4. Pace, loudness and pitch: too fast or slow, too loud or flat, too high or low?
5. Would you keep listening, keep the screen on, or close it? At what second?
6. One thing that would make you stay.

Then: which clip would you put on tomorrow night, and why?

Be blunt. Praise is useless to us; honest reactions are what we need.

## Output

Write `REVIEW_CHATGPT.md` in this folder (`reviews/004-first-listener/`). Under 800 words.
