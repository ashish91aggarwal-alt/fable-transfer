# Review brief for ChatGPT — package 002 (pilot sleep story, script stage)

Review the first pilot video for the new cozy sleep-story channel (plan: `reviews/001-channel-plan/`). This package covers the script and scene plan only. Voice, pictures and cost logs come in a later package.

Files (in `channels/sleep-stories/001-lighthouse-inn/`):
- `script_full.txt` — the full narration (4,672 words). About 50–60 min at a slow sleep pace of 85–100 words per minute, including pauses.
- `script_part1.txt` to `script_part4.txt` — the same text in four parts.
- `SCENES.md` — the 26 planned pictures and the channel's style.

Audience: adults in the UK and US who use sleep stories to fall asleep. The story should be calm, free of peril, slow, and sensory, and become gradually sleepier towards the end.

## Review these questions

1. **Sleep fitness:** Does anything risk waking or alerting a listener (tension, surprise, sharp sounds, too much plot, questions to the listener)? Quote the lines.
2. **Pacing:** Does it slow down enough towards the end? Is the opening breathing section the right length?
3. **Originality:** Does it read as distinct, or as generic AI sleep-story writing? Mark the most generic passages and suggest how to make them more specific.
4. **Ear-friendliness:** Lines that are hard to read aloud, tongue-twisters, or phrases a text-to-speech voice may mispronounce (names, "Saltmere", etc.).
5. **Length:** Is 50–60 minutes the right length for a first video, or should it be longer (the channel needs watch hours)? If longer, what would you add that is not padding?
6. **Scenes:** Are 26 pictures enough for this length? Which scenes are weak or hard to generate consistently?
7. **Title and thumbnail idea:** Rate "A Rainy Night at the Lighthouse Inn | Cozy Sleep Story for Grown-Ups" and suggest two alternatives and one thumbnail concept.
8. **Anything else.**

## Output

Write `REVIEW_CHATGPT.md` in this folder (`reviews/002-pilot/`), structured as:

```
# ChatGPT review — package 002
## Verdict (2–3 sentences)
## Top 5 changes, most important first (each: change, why, confidence)
## Answers to questions 1–8 (quote line text, not line numbers)
```

Keep it under about 1,500 words.
