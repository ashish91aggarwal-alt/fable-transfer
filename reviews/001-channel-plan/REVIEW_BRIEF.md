# Review brief for ChatGPT — package 001 (channel plan)

You are reviewing a YouTube channel plan written by Claude. Read `PLAN.md` in this folder. Be a demanding, independent critic: your job is to find what is wrong, weak or missing, not to praise it.

## Review these questions

1. **Deadline math:** Is reaching 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours on a brand-new channel by 31 Jan 2027 realistic with this cadence? If not, what would change the odds most?
2. **Niche choice:** Are cozy sleep stories (UK/US) and American frontier history (US) the best first two picks for AI-produced, tier-1, long-watch-time channels? Name any niche you think beats them, with evidence or reasoning.
3. **Policy risk:** Where could these channels fall foul of YouTube's inauthentic (mass-produced) content rules or AI disclosure rules? What concrete practices keep them safe?
4. **Money:** Are the RPM and earnings ranges sensible for these niches and countries? Flag anything over-optimistic.
5. **Workflow and cost:** Is under $5 per video and $60/month realistic for open-weight voice and images on rented GPUs? Any bottleneck or quality risk?
6. **Titles:** Rate the 12 draft titles and rewrite the weakest three.
7. **Analytics loop:** Are the kill/scale thresholds and review timings sensible? What is missing?
8. **Anything else** you would change.

If you rely on facts that may have changed (YouTube policy, thresholds), say how sure you are and what you would check.

## Output

Write one Markdown file named `REVIEW_CHATGPT.md`, structured as:

```
# ChatGPT review — package 001
## Verdict (2–3 sentences)
## Top 5 changes, most important first
   (each: the change, why, confidence high/medium/low)
## Answers to questions 1–8
## Anything I could not judge, and what I would need
```

Keep it under about 1,500 words. Put the file in this same folder: `reviews/001-channel-plan/REVIEW_CHATGPT.md`.
