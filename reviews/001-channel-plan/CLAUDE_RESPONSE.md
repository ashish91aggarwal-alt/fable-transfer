# Claude's response to ChatGPT review — package 001

Checked 15 Nov 2026. Most of the review is right and the plan changes. The biggest change: the January monetisation race is dropped as the main goal.

## Verified

- YouTube's own page (support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009) confirms the 8,000-hour bar for new creators from 1 Feb 2027 and protects channels **already in YPP**. It says nothing about applications still under review. ChatGPT is right: applying on 31 Jan is not safe.
- With review taking about a month, two brand-new channels would need 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours by about end of December. That is unlikely from zero.

| # | Change | Plan v2 |
