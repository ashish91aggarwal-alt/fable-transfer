## Continuity reference (lock before generating)

- White round tower, black lantern top, beam to seaward; long white single-storey inn with slate roof beside it, blue gate, low stone wall.
- Tower is about 30 m from the inn across a small yard; Mara's attic window faces the tower.
- Lens: tall beehive of glass prisms, the same in scenes 19–21.
- Biscuit: one large grey cat, golden eyes.
- Weather: soft steady rain, never storm; mist in the tower scenes; clearing cloud only from scene 21.
- No readable text anywhere in images; any title is added in editing.
- Transitions: slow crossfades only; slow push-ins, no quick moves.

## Sound plan

- Continuous low rain throughout; very soft sea; quiet fire in the inn scenes.
- No bell, latch, thunder, strong wind or sharp fire cracks as sound effects.
- No music sting or loud intro. Optional very soft pad under the opening, faded out by scene 3.
- After narration: 10–20 min of rain and soft sea over the final dimming image, stated in the description and chapters.
