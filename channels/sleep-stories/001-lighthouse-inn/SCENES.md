# Lighthouse Inn: scene list (draft)

Style for the whole channel (to be approved): soft painterly illustration, muted dusk palette (slate blue, warm amber, cream), gentle film grain, no people's faces in close-up, no text in images, 16:9. Each scene is held for 1.5–3 minutes with a slow camera move (push-in or drift), plus rain and fire ambience.
