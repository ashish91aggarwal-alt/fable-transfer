Mara's eyes were heavy now. She let them close.

She listened to the rain on the roof.

She listened to the far, soft hush of the sea, breathing in, and breathing out.

She felt the warmth at her feet, and the weight of the quilt.

She thought about the little footbridge over the stream, where the raindrops made rings on the water. The rings spreading out, and meeting, and fading.

Spreading out. And meeting. And fading.

She thought about the steps, going around and around.

She thought about the boats far out on the dark water, moving slowly home.

And the light came around again, across the ceiling. Slow, and soft, and pale. And faded. And came again.

The map could wait for the morning. The path would still be there.

For now, there was only the rain. And the sea. And the light, going round.

Patter, patter, on the roof.

Breathing in. And out.

The light, going round.

Warm. And still.

The light, going round.

And round.

And round.

Sleep well.
