[softly] Mara's eyes were heavy now. [gently] She let them close.

She listened to the rain on the roof... [whispers] patter... patter...

She listened to the far, soft hush of the sea... breathing in... and breathing out.

[softly] She felt the warmth at her feet... and the weight of the quilt, holding her.

She thought about the little footbridge over the stream, where the raindrops made rings on the water. The rings spreading out... and meeting... and fading.

[slowly] Spreading out. And meeting. And fading.

And the light came around again, across the ceiling. Slow... and soft... and pale. [whispers] And it faded. And it came again.

[softly] The map could wait for the morning. The path would still be there.

For now... there was only the rain. And the sea. And the light, going round.

[whispers] And round... and round.

[softly] Sleep well.
