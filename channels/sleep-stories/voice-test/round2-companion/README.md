# Round 2: companion delivery

Owner feedback on round 1: every voice sounded like a narrator reading a book: no feeling, no rise and fall, no blending, not human.

Changes in round 2:
- ElevenLabs v3 (expressive model) with emotion tags in the script: [softly], [warmly], [whispers], [exhales softly].
- Whole passage generated in one go, so the voice flows between paragraphs (round 1 stitched paragraphs separately, which reset the intonation each time).
- Script rewritten to speak *with* the listener ("take a breath in with me", "you and I"), with ellipses for natural trailing pauses.
- Stability 0.5 (more variation than round 1's 0.6 on the older model).
- Two US and two UK voices, labelled by accent.

Measured pitch movement (opening): round 1 voices 2.7–4.7 semitones spread; round 2 4.0–6.6 (Sarah the most varied).
