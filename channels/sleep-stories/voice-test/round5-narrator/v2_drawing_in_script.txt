Picture a path. A pale little path, running along the very top of the cliffs, like a ribbon someone dropped in the grass and forgot about. On your left, fields of heather and gorse, rolling away, brown and gold. And on your right... far below... the sea. Grey as a pigeon's wing. Breathing in, and out, against the rocks.

And there, walking along it, is Mara. She's been out since the middle of the afternoon, and her cheeks are pink from the wind. Mara makes maps. All autumn she's been walking this coast with a notebook in her pocket, drawing every little cove, every stile, every crooked old signpost. And she loves it. Because you can't hurry a map, you see. You have to walk every single step of it yourself.
