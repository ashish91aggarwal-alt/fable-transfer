# Winter fireplace videos (draft plan)

Runs next to the Lighthouse Inn pilot. Winter means more "cozy fireplace" sleep searches.

## Two video types

| Type | Length | What it is | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fireplace sound | 2–3 h | Crackling fire (+ optional winter wind), one painted fireplace scene with gentle flicker, no voice | Free (sound made in code; picture from the image model) |
| Winter sleep story | 45–60 min | Story by a fire, narrator + soft character voices, fire sound under the voice | Voice render on Runpod (cents) |

## First winter story ideas

1. Snowfall at the Woodland Cabin
2. A Winter Night at the Mountain Lodge
3. Christmas Eve at the Old Bookshop
