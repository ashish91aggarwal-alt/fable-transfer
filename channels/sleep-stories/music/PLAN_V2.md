# Music plan v2 (after ChatGPT review 003)

Replaces section 6–8 of RESEARCH.md. Music is an experiment in listener comfort, not a sleep treatment.

## What changed

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| 60–80 bpm and 30–45 min stated as proven | Treated as common practice, not proven optimum. Evidence is moderate only for *how well people feel they slept* |
| Music always on | Music is optional. First test rain-only against two music versions |
| -20 to -24 dB as a rule | A starting point, measured as short-term loudness (LUFS) of voice vs. music; then checked by ear on phone speaker and earbuds at bedtime volume |
| Music rises 3–4 dB in pauses | No pause lift. Slow, invisible level changes only |
| 20-second channel tune | At most a 3–8 s, very quiet cue that dissolves before the story; tested, not mandatory |
| Tanpura = "one steady tone" | Corrected: tanpura is a shimmering, overtone-rich drone on the tonic and fifth. We use a *soft warm pad inspired by it*, not real tanpura buzz |
| Instruments chosen by nationality | Chosen by how they sound, tested blind. For this UK-coast story, rain and room warmth matter more than any national instrument |
| "AI music is not in Content ID" | Not relied on. Every asset logged; every video uploaded privately first to check for claims |

## The test (no paid spend)

A 10-minute excerpt of the real story (part 2: arrival, supper by the fire), voice-2 narration, three versions:

- **A:** voice + rain
- **B:** A + soft low drone (tanpura-inspired pad)
- **C:** B + a few slow piano/harp notes in the quiet gaps

All three matched in overall loudness. Owner listens on phone speaker and earbuds and notes: what you noticed, anything that hid words, what felt warm, anything irritating.

Gates for any version to pass: voice always clear; no seams or loop clicks; no deep rumble; works on one earbud and in mono; graceful ending.

## Rights ledger (kept per video)

Everything is synthesised from code (no sample libraries, soundfonts or recorded rain), so there are no third-party licences. The ledger records: the script that made each sound, settings and seed, date, and the private-upload Content ID result. No GPU spend unless a code-made version beats rain-only.
