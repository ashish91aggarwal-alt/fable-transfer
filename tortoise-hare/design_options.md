# Design options (text concepts, nothing painted yet)

Rules for every option: kind faces, readable at phone size, no scary looks, flaws only by behavior, one checkable accessory, colors far apart between the three characters.

## Tilly the tortoise
1. **Moss gardener:** wide, low shell with a tiny moss garden, two clover leaves and one small mushroom growing on top. Snub nose, sleepy-kind eyes.
2. **Patchwork quilt:** shell made of stitched cloth-like squares in mismatched colors, round wire glasses, wool socks on all four feet.
3. **River pebble hiker:** smooth grey-blue shell like a stone, tiny canvas backpack, a leaf-brim sun hat, a walking twig.
4. **Little one in boots:** a baby-proportioned tortoise with an oversized head, tiny shell, big yellow rain boots and a paper sun hat.
5. **Wise grandmother:** older and rounder, a crocheted shawl, half-moon spectacles, a shell with rings like a tree stump.
6. **Folk-art embroidered:** shell covered in bold block-print flowers and diamonds in teal, coral and mustard, with a matching flower tucked behind one eye.
7. **Sunset geometric:** very simple rounded shapes, a shell in layered watercolor sunset bands, striped stockings, big round eyes.
8. **Scarf-and-snail friend:** green tortoise with a long striped knitted scarf trailing behind her, a tiny snail riding on the shell as a quiet companion who never speaks.

## Hugo the hare
1. **Lanky athlete:** very long limbs, a number-one bib on his chest, a sweatband, knee-high striped socks.
2. **Puffball:** round fluffy body, tiny long legs, a wild tuft of hair on his head, round aviator goggles pushed up on his forehead.
3. **Big sneakers:** an ordinary lean hare with huge red sneakers and white laces, nothing else, so the shoes carry the "fast" idea.
4. **Spotted Dutch hare:** black-and-white patches with a patch over one eye, a bell-less ribbon collar, droopy half-folded ears.
5. **Snow hare:** white and blue-grey fur with black ear tips, a long orange scarf that streams behind when he runs, bright orange nose.
6. **Cape kid:** a small-framed hare in a homemade red cape pinned with a button, pretend superhero, freckles and one missing front tooth.
7. **Felt-doll hare:** looks sewn from felt, visible stitching, button eyes with a highlight, mismatched ears (one up, one flopped).
8. **Dandy hare:** tall and thin, a green waistcoat with brass buttons, a jaunty feather in a small cap, an air of showing off.

## Wren / watcher (one small non-speaking character on the fence)
1. **Classic wren:** tiny round brown bird with an upturned tail and a loud-looking open beak when singing.
2. **Race announcer:** a wren in a tiny knitted yellow hat holding a small checkered flag on a stick.
3. **Fluffball chick:** an oversized-head, fluffy fledgling in cream and pale brown, a single tuft on the head.
4. **Leaf-umbrella wren:** a brown wren sheltering under a large leaf held like an umbrella, a speckled chest.
5. **Ladybug spectator:** a round red ladybug with black dots and a tiny polka-dot flag.
6. **Field mouse:** a small grey mouse sitting on the fence with a wooden ratchet and a daisy behind one ear.
7. **Dragonfly:** a slender teal dragonfly with glassy wings hovering over the finish ribbon.
8. **Bluebird:** a rounder blue-and-peach bird with a teal scarf, so the color is clear of Hugo's red.
